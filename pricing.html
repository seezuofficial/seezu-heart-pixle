Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our pricing and services

What's included in the price?

All prices include design, development, testing, and deployment. You'll receive a complete, functional website. Domain and hosting are separate but we can help you set them up.

How long does it take?

Emotional websites: 3-5 days. Business websites: 7-14 days depending on complexity. We'll provide a specific timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer a 50% advance, 50% on completion payment structure for most projects. For larger projects, we can discuss custom payment plans.

What about revisions?

We include 2 rounds of design revisions and 1 round of development revisions. We work closely with you to ensure the final product matches your vision perfectly.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade your plan at any time. We'll apply what you've already paid toward the new plan.

Do you offer discounts?

We offer special discounts for students, non-profits, and bulk orders (multiple websites). Contact us via WhatsApp to discuss.

Need a Custom Solution?

These packages are starting points. Most projects are unique. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your specific needs.
