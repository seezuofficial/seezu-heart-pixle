For emotional websites: stories, photos, favorite colors/music. For business websites: brand details, target audience, competitors. The more details, the better we can serve you.

Yes! We offer a free 30-minute consultation call to understand your needs and provide initial recommendations. No obligation to proceed.

Emotional websites: 3-5 days. Business websites: 7-14 days. Complex projects may take longer. We'll provide a specific timeline after our initial discussion.

Absolutely! We work with clients worldwide. All communication happens online, and we're experienced in managing projects across different time zones.
